Radiology Department Overview

Our Radiology division leverages cutting-edge low-dose X-ray machines, advanced high-field MRI systems, multi-slice CT scanners, and 4D ultrasound suites to produce crisp internal structural readouts interpreted by experienced radiologists.

Pathology Department Overview

The Pathology department features fully automated analyzers for routine and complex hematology parameters, deep biochemical profile evaluations, and accurate microscopic tissue analysis (histopathology) to confirm clinical diagnosis swiftly.

Microbiology Department Overview

Dedicated to detecting infectious agents, our microbiology suite uses state-of-the-art incubation and PCR platforms for bacterial culture testing and viral identification, empowering clinicians with precise antimicrobial susceptibility markers.
